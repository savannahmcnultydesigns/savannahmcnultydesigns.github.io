About Me

Hi there! I’m Savannah.

I’m a broadcast design leader with 7+ years of experience building teams, streamlining creative operations, and creating high-volume graphics in fast-paced, 24/7 news environments.

I bring strong design skills to the table, but I also believe great creatives never stop learning. I encourage my teams to try new things, build new skills, and keep pushing themselves, because that’s where better ideas and stronger creative work can blossom from.

I believe the best teams are built by investing in the people who make them unique. Hire talented people for what they bring to the table, give them room to develop, and create an environment where they can do their best work. When people feel supported and challenged, everyone benefits.
